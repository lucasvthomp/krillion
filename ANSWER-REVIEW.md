# Krillion answer bank review

Reviewed **923 prompts** and tested **4,906 guesses** against the game’s matcher. This pass updated **445 banks** with **1,407 answers** and **587 alternate names**. All reviewed guesses now score. The **815 bold guesses** were rejected in the [previous build](https://github.com/lucasvthomp/krillion/commit/9a0e0d04783e892c2e09b63ddef7e0fe20944e6c).

Each row records the guesses checked for that prompt and why the bank changed or stayed as it was. The checks cover selected answers for every prompt. Open categories can have additional valid answers, and time-sensitive membership can change after review. Any complete roster or catalog would need to match the prompt’s wording and date cutoff.
