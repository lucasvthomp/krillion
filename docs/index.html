          How to play

          1. Each dive draws seven different prompts from the answer bank.
          2. Read each prompt for three seconds, then take up to 25 seconds to answer.
          3. Wrong guesses carry no time penalty. You can guess again or pass.
          4. Short forms count, such as USA for United States. For people, a last name is enough.
          5. If your answer is close to one or more answers, pick the one you meant while the timer runs.
          6. Each point takes you 10 metres deeper.
